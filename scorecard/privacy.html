Privacy Policy

Effective for Scorepad Version 1.1 and later

Scorepad is designed to help you keep score for card, board, and tabletop games without sacrificing your privacy. We built Scorepad to operate completely offline with zero accounts, zero ads, and zero tracking.

1. Zero Data Collection

Scorepad does not collect, transmit, rent, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, or device identifiers. We operate no backend servers, require no sign-in, and have no access to anything you enter in the app.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All data you create in Scorepad stays privately on your iPhone:

  • Games, Players & Presets: Game sessions, round scores, saved player profiles, Game Night series, and custom presets are stored locally in your device's encrypted container using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as appearance mode, haptics, sound effects, keep-screen-awake, and StoreKit review prompt timing are stored locally on your device via Apple's standard UserDefaults API.

3. Widgets, Live Activities, Siri & Voice Readout

  • Widgets & Live Activities: Lock Screen Live Activities, Dynamic Island views, Home Screen widgets, and Control Center controls render strictly on your device using Apple's ActivityKit and WidgetKit frameworks. Scorepad does not use remote push notifications.
  • Siri & App Shortcuts: Siri Shortcuts run locally on your device through Apple's App Intents framework.
  • Read Scores Aloud: Optional voice announcements are generated entirely on your device using Apple's AVSpeechSynthesizer. Scorepad never accesses your microphone or records audio.

4. Optional User-Controlled Backups

In Settings, you may optionally export a JSON backup file of your games and player profiles or restore a backup file you previously saved. Backup files are saved only to the destination you explicitly choose in Apple's system document picker (such as On My iPhone or your personal iCloud Drive) and are never sent to us.

5. Third-Party SDKs & Tracking

Scorepad contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporters, or trackers. The app is built exclusively with Apple's native iOS frameworks.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Scorepad collects no personal information from any user, it complies with applicable children's privacy laws (including COPPA and GDPR-K) and carries a 4+ age rating on the App Store.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever revise this Privacy Policy for future app capabilities, we will publish the updated text on this page prior to release.

8. Contact Us
