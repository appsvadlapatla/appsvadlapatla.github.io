Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start or finish a game?

On the Games tab, tap any built-in game preset (such as Uno, Catan, Spades, Hearts, Rummy, Skyjo, or Scrabble) or tap + New Game. While scoring, tap Next Round to advance rounds or tap Finish at the bottom of the scoreboard anytime to crown the winner.

How do I fix a past round score or delete an old game?

Inside any scoreboard, switch to the Sheet tab and tap any cell to edit a past round score, or tap the undo arrow in the top bar. To remove a match, open the History tab and swipe left on any game to delete it (with an instant Undo button).

How do I edit saved players?

Open the Players tab to add, rename, customize, or archive players, as well as view career win rates, streaks, and head-to-head rivalries.

Does Scorepad require an internet connection or account?

No. Scorepad works 100% offline with zero accounts, zero ads, and zero subscriptions, whether you are at the kitchen table, camping, or on a flight.

How do I back up or transfer my game history?

Open Settings (the gear icon on the Games tab) and tap Save Backup to export a private JSON file of all your games, players, and custom presets to Files or iCloud Drive. Tap Restore Backup anytime on a new iPhone.
