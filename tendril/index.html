Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · Applies to Tendril (com.abhinav.tendril) and Tendril Widgets (com.abhinav.tendril.widgets)

Tendril does not operate user accounts, remote databases, advertising networks, crash telemetry SDKs, or third-party analytics. Every budget, expense, receipt scan, voice entry, and savings goal stays strictly on your iPhone.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Tendril declares NSPrivacyTracking = false and NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes = [] in its Apple Privacy Manifest. We never collect, transmit, rent, or sell:

  • Personal identifiers, names, email addresses, or user accounts
  • Bank credentials, card numbers, or financial institution tokens
  • Transactions, merchants, receipts, voice recordings, or savings goals
  • Usage analytics, IP addresses, or device fingerprinting signals

2. Local Storage, App Group & Biometric Lock

  • Your budgets, entries, goals, and preferences are stored solely on your iPhone in an encrypted local SwiftData database protected with FileProtectionType.completeUntilFirstUserAuthentication.
  • A lightweight read-only snapshot is shared privately inside your device's encrypted App Group container (group.com.abhinav.tendril) so your Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets, and Control Center buttons display up-to-date figures without launching the main app.
  • Optional Face ID or Touch ID locking uses Apple's on-device LocalAuthentication framework and masks presented sheets in the iOS App Switcher. Tendril never accesses or stores biometric data.

3. On-Device Camera, Photos, PDF & Speech Processing

  • Camera & Photos: When you scan a paper receipt or import an image or PDF, Tendril parses it locally on your iPhone using Apple Vision text recognition, PDFKit, and on-device Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models) to pre-fill merchant, date, category, and line items. Original images and PDFs are never uploaded to external servers.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: Spoken entries are transcribed locally on-device using Apple's Speech framework solely to populate an expense draft for your confirmation.

4. Exports, Backups & Complete Deletion

  • You can export your ledger anytime as CSV, JSON, a Check-In summary card (with an option to hide exact amounts), or a printable one-page Monthly Almanac PDF through the standard iOS Share sheet.
  • Deleting entries inside Tendril removes them immediately from your local database. You can also erase all local data in Settings, and uninstalling Tendril removes its database and App Group container from your iPhone.

5. Children, Contact & Policy Updates

Tendril collects zero personal information from users of any age (rated 4+). If a future update ever introduces optional cloud features, this policy will be updated clearly before any change takes effect. Privacy questions can be emailed to avadlapatla.apps+tendril@gmail.com.

Disclaimer: Tendril is a personal budgeting tool and does not provide financial, tax, or investment advice.

Support & Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guides for receipt scanning, voice entry, waterfall savings goals, backups, and widgets.

How do I scan receipts from Camera, Photos, or PDF files?
  1. Tap and hold the green + button in the dock (or tap the scanner icon inside Quick Add) and choose Scan Receipt.
  2. Frame a paper receipt in the live viewfinder, tap the folder icon at bottom-left to import from Photos or Files (PDF/images), or tap the clipboard icon at top-right to paste a screenshot.
  3. On-device Vision OCR and Apple Intelligence extract the merchant, date, total, and line items. Uncheck excluded items or choose a split (Full, ÷2, ÷3, ÷4) before saving.
How does offline Voice Entry work?

Tap and hold + and select Voice (or launch Voice Entry from Control Center or Siri), then speak naturally such as "Fourteen dollars for coffee at Blue Bottle". Transcription and parsing run locally on your iPhone.

How do Waterfall Savings Goals work?

Open the Goals tab and rank your savings goals in priority order. Any unspent monthly budget fills Priority 1 first; once Priority 1 reaches 100%, remaining surplus cascades automatically into Priority 2 and then Priority 3. Press and drag any basin to reorder priorities anytime.

How do I import CSV statements or export backups and PDFs?
  • CSV & JSON Import/Export: Open Settings (gear icon) to import Apple Card or bank CSV statements, restore a Tendril JSON backup, or export clean CSV spreadsheets and full JSON backups.
  • Monthly Almanac PDF & Check-In Card: Tap the Share button from Insights or open Monthly Almanac & Check-In in Settings to export a one-page US Letter PDF or a shareable summary card with optional hidden amounts.
How do I enable Face ID or Touch ID lock?

Open Settings and toggle Face ID Lock on. Tendril automatically locks when you leave the app, masks open sheets in the App Switcher, and redacts sensitive figures on widgets and Spotlight search.

How do I add Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets, or Control Center actions?
  • Widgets: Long-press your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, and search for Tendril to add Botanical Canopy or Waterfall Savings Goal widgets.
  • Control Center: Open Control Center, tap + (top-left), choose Add a Control, and search Tendril to pin Quick Add, Scan Receipt, or Voice Entry.
