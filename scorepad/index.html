Support & Guide VERSION 2.0

Getting Started at the Table

  • Five-Second New Pad: Choose from 22 built-in public-domain formats (Cribbage, Spades, Hearts, Euchre, Gin Rummy, Dominoes, Golf, Darts 501, Mahjong, Pickleball, Five-Dice Yacht, Six-Dice 10,000, and more) or start a custom pad right away.
  • Interactive Paper Scorepad: Pinned player totals sit at the top with tie-aware rank tags and a live leader strip. Tap any round cell to open the inline mechanical keypad and quick nudge keys (-5, -1, +1, +5, +10), then tap Next to glide across the row.
  • Built for Big Groups (5 to 16 Players): Games with five or more players automatically open in an adaptive vertical ledger with a round selector strip, full-width ranked rows, and one-tap Grid/List and Seat/Rank toggles.
  • Counter Mode: Use simple counter scoring for fast games with direct minus and plus buttons right on each player row.

Mid-Game Edits, Archive & Tabletop Tools

  • Add Player & Edit Pad Mid-Game: Open the ... menu inside any pad to add a latecomer (with an optional catch-up score), rename players, change ink colors, or update target scores and winning rules without restarting.
  • Archive Gestures: Tap < Pads to view your active pad and chronological history. On past pads, swipe right to start a quick Rematch or swipe left to Delete (with an Undo toast).
  • Lock Screen & Tabletop Tools: Glance at live standings and tap +1 Point or Next Round from Lock Screen Live Activities and Dynamic Island, or open Tools for precision Dice, Coin Flip, Turn Timer, and Who's First.
  • Backups & Direct Help: Export or restore local JSON backups anytime in Settings. Need help or want to report an issue? Email avadlapatla.apps+scorepad@gmail.com with your iOS version and a short note.
Frequently Asked Questions QUICK ANSWERS
Can I add a player after a game has already started?

Yes. Open the ... menu at the top right of your scorepad and tap Add Player. You can pick from saved players or enter a new name, choose an ink color, and optionally enter a catch-up score for missed rounds.

How do I switch between Grid and List layouts for big groups?

Games with five or more players open in the vertical List ledger automatically so every player and rank stays clearly visible. You can tap the Grid / List or Seat / Rank toggle in the top standings strip anytime.

How do I delete or rematch a past pad, or delete a running game?

On the Pads archive screen, swipe right on any past pad to reveal Rematch, or swipe left to reveal Delete (with an Undo toast). To delete a currently active pad, tap the ... menu on the Active Pad card or inside the scorepad and choose Delete Pad.

Does Scorepad work completely offline and without an account?

Yes. Scorepad requires no account, no subscription, and no internet connection. All pads, player stats, and custom setups stay on your iPhone, and you can export or restore local JSON backups anytime in Settings.

Privacy Policy EFFECTIVE NOV 22, 2026

1. Data Not Collected

Scorepad stores all game sessions, player names, ink preferences, custom presets, and settings strictly on your device using Apple SwiftData protected at rest with iOS Data Protection. We do not operate servers that receive or read your data, and Scorepad includes zero accounts, zero analytics, zero crash-reporting SDKs, and zero advertising trackers.

100% On-Device No Accounts No Tracking No Ads

2. Optional Device Permissions

  • Photos (Add Only): Requested only if you explicitly choose "Save to Photos" when exporting a match recap card. Scorepad never reads your photo library.
  • Live Activities, Widgets & Siri Shortcuts: Controlled through iOS Settings and run locally between Scorepad and Apple system surfaces on your device.

3. Exports, Deletion & Contact

You stay in full control of your data. Exports happen only when you initiate a local JSON backup in Settings or share a recap card through the iOS Share Sheet. Deleting Scorepad or clearing matches inside the app permanently removes your local database.
