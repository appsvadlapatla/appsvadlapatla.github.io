Privacy Policy

A quiet, private baby logbook designed for your family's earliest days.

📱

Stays on Your iPhone

Everything you log, including nursing sessions, bottles, expressed milk, diapers, sleep, solids, growth check-ins, health notes, and baby photos, stays strictly on your iPhone. We never transmit or store your data on external servers.

✋

No Accounts, Ads, or Tracking

Burp has no sign-ups, analytics, advertising SDKs, or subscriptions. We never sell or share your family's data because we never collect or see it.

🔒

Protected on Device

Your logs are stored locally on your iPhone and protected by your device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. Built-in brown noise in Sleep mode is generated offline on your iPhone with no internet connection.

🔔

Optional Permissions

Local notifications are completely optional, configured independently per baby, and only used for reminders you turn on. Choosing a baby photo uses Apple's system photo picker without granting access to your full photo library.

🩺

Medical Disclaimer

Burp is a personal family logbook, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice, percentiles, diagnosis, or treatment. Health temperatures are strictly manual user-entered logs. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before making any medical decisions or with questions regarding your child's feeding, growth, temperature, or medications.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch units, customize Swaddle Bean, or enable 3 AM Red-Shift?

Tap Nursery in the top-left corner of Home to switch between Imperial and Metric units, turn on 3 AM Red-Shift amber mode (or schedule it automatically from 10 PM to 6 AM), manage per-baby reminders, or replay the interactive App Tour. Tap your baby's name pill in the top-right corner to edit your baby's profile or customize Swaddle Bean's skin tone, hair, swaddle color, or photo.

How do twins and per-baby reminders work?

Add multiple babies from the Nursery drawer. Each baby keeps their own independent Home feed dock preference (Nurse or Bottle, Breast Milk or Formula), Swaddle Bean appearance, and per-baby reminder switches.

Can I share a shift summary, print a PDF, or merge backups with my partner?

Yes. Swipe up to the Care Tray and tap Share (or open Nursery) to send a one-tap Shift Handoff postcard or export a 1-page Care Summary PDF (7, 14, or 30 days). You can also export or restore portable JSON backups anytime; partner merges skip duplicates automatically.

Does Burp support Live Activities, Apple Watch, Widgets, and Siri Shortcuts?

Yes. Active nursing and sleep timers appear automatically on your Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, StandBy, and Apple Watch Smart Stack so you can switch sides or end a session without unlocking. You can also add Home Screen Widgets, Control Center buttons, Action Button controls, and use Siri Shortcuts ("Start nursing in Burp", "Start sleep in Burp", or "Log a wet diaper in Burp").

How do I edit or delete an entry or profile?

Swipe up to open the Care Tray and scroll to Today's Story or tap Journal to browse, edit, or delete any logged entry. To edit or remove a baby profile, open the Nursery drawer and tap the edit button next to that baby.
