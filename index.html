BURP Privacy Policy

Your privacy is built into the core of BURP. We believe your family's data belongs to you, and nobody else.

📱

Local Storage

All data you log, including feeding times, diaper changes, growth, health notes, and baby profiles, is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your data on external servers.

✋

No Tracking

BURP does not use third-party tracking or analytics. We don't sell your data because we never even see it.

🔒

Secure by Design

By keeping everything offline, your family's information stays where it belongs: with you. We recommend using a device passcode (FaceID/TouchID) to further protect your baby's logs.

🔔

Permissions

We only ask for notification permissions to provide you with helpful reminders. This permission is completely optional and can be managed in your device settings at any time.

🩺

Medical Disclaimer

BURP is a personal tracking and organizational logbook, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your pediatrician or qualified healthcare provider with any questions regarding your child's health, feeding, growth, temperature, or medications.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change units?
Go to Settings in the app to switch between Metric and Imperial units.

Can I back up or export my data?
Yes! Open Settings and tap Export Activities (CSV) or Export Full Backup (JSON) to save or share your records anywhere.

How do I edit or delete data?
Tap any logged activity to edit or delete it, or open Settings (or long-press a profile in the baby switcher) to edit or delete a baby's profile.
