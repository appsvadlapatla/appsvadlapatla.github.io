Privacy Policy

Designed as a calm daily logbook for your family's earliest days.

📱

100% Local Storage

All data you log, including feeding times, expressed milk, diaper changes, sleep, growth measurements, health notes, and baby portraits, is stored locally on your iPhone inside iOS Data Protection containers. We never transmit or store your data on external servers.

✋

No Accounts, No Analytics

Burp contains zero third-party SDKs, zero analytics, and zero advertising trackers. We never sell your data because we never see it.

🔒

Encrypted at Rest

By keeping everything offline on your device, your family's records stay where they belong: with you. Using a device passcode (Face ID or Touch ID) protects your baby's logs automatically.

🔔

Optional Permissions

Notification permission is completely optional and used solely to schedule local, on-device reminders. Photo access uses Apple's private system picker only when you choose a portrait for your baby.

🩺

Medical Disclaimer

Burp is a personal tracking and organizational logbook, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your pediatrician or qualified healthcare provider with any questions regarding your child's health, feeding, growth, temperature, or medications.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch units or enable 3 AM Red-Shift mode?
Open the More tab to switch between Metric and Imperial units, choose Light, Night, or 3 AM Red-Shift OLED appearance, or tap the moon button in the top-right header on Today.

Can I share a shift summary or merge backups with my partner?
Yes! Tap the share button on Today (or Share Partner Shift Handoff in More) to send a 1-tap summary postcard. For full backups or moving to a new iPhone, use Create Full Backup and Restore Backup in More (duplicates are skipped automatically).

Does Burp support Lock Screen Live Activities, Widgets, and Siri?
Yes! Active nursing and sleep timers appear automatically on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets display live care status, and you can say "Siri, start nursing in Burp", "Siri, start sleep in Burp", or "Siri, log a wet diaper in Burp" hands-free.

How do I edit or delete an entry or profile?
Tap any logged activity in Today, Journal, or Trends to edit or delete it, or open More to edit or remove a baby's profile.
